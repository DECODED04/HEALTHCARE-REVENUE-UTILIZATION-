# Healthcare Revenue & Utilization Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes healthcare revenue and service utilization across different encounter types and insurance payers. The analysis explores revenue performance, patient encounters, insurance coverage, mortality patterns, and changes in revenue over time.

## Business Question

How do encounter types and insurance payers influence healthcare revenue, utilization, mortality, and coverage, and how has revenue changed over time?

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Excel Slicers

## Key Insights

- The healthcare system generated approximately **$101.5 million in total revenue** across about **27,000 encounters**.
- **Ambulatory encounters generated the highest revenue** among the encounter classes analyzed, followed by emergency and inpatient encounters.
- Revenue contribution varied considerably across insurance payers, with **UnitedHealthcare generating the highest payer revenue** on the dashboard.
- A noticeable portion of encounters had **zero insurance coverage**, indicating that some healthcare services were delivered without insurance contribution.
- Revenue generally increased across several years before showing a substantial decline in **2022**. This should be interpreted cautiously until it is confirmed that 2022 contains a complete year of data.
